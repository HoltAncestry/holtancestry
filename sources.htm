Sources

The history of the Holt family has been carefully pieced together using a variety of ancestral records, rare books, and digital archives. These sources represent years of dedicated research into our past and are shared here to assist fellow genealogists. If you have discovered additional records or have questions about the materials listed, please feel free to get in touch.

We continually update our source list as new historical records become digitized and available.

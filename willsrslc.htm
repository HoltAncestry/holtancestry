Probate Records (Wills & Administrations)

Complied from the Record Society of Lancashire and Cheshire (RSLC)

These details are extracted from published indexes and calendars. While they are comprehensive for basic identification, the sources emphasize that more information is found in the original documents (wills, administrations, and inventories):

  • Act Books : These record details not always present in the will itself, including the testator’s date of death and the assessed value of the personal estate.
  • Infra wills : An asterisk or italicised entry usually marks an infra will, meaning the personal estate was valued at under £40.
  • Record types : Ad. or Admon. indicates an administration bond issued when no will existed, while Tuition refers to a guardianship bond for minor children.

Accessibility Statement

HoltAncestry aims to be usable by as many visitors as possible. The site is built as a simple, static resource with clear text, straightforward navigation, and minimal interactive elements. Pages are designed to work on a range of devices, including mobile screens. If you encounter any difficulty accessing content or reading the material, please contact us and we will do our best to provide the information in an alternative format.

Privacy Notice

HoltAncestry is a simple, static website that does not collect personal information from visitors. There are no accounts, logins, subscriptions, or interactive features. All genealogical material relates solely to historical individuals who are long deceased.

Please share your details if you have any further information about the Holt families of Lancashire or other Holt families. This site focuses on the UK, but wider international families may have connections. I am interested in any details you may have found or have stored in your attic relating to the Holt family name: births, marriages, deaths, burials, houses, tales, pedigrees, heraldry, newspaper cuttings, wills, DNA-linked histories or other sources of information.

If you do have resources that I haven't yet unearthed, please send your contributions. These will be much appreciated. Please let me have any comments, feedback or additions.

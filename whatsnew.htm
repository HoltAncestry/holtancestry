What's New at Holt Ancestry

Track our latest archival transcriptions, DNA project discoveries, and regional family histories.

May 2026

  • New Dataset: Transcribed the 1642 Rochdale Protestation Returns, isolating all Holt males listed in the parish.
  • Location Study: Added a deep dive into Castleton Hall and the 16th-century descendants of Adam Holt.
  • Socio-Economic History: Published a new piece on the Enclosure Movement and its effects on Thomas Holt’s (d. 1494) estate.
  • Biographical Dictionary: Significantly expanded the Biographies section with deep-dive profiles of notable individuals, charting their specific impacts on military, religious, and legal history.
  • Heraldry & Lineage: Updated the Pedigree & Heraldry page to analyze the official coats of arms, shields, and quarterings recorded in the Heralds' Visitations for the primary branches (including Stubley and Gristlehurst).

April 2026

  • Tax Records: Completed the comparative analysis of the Lancashire Hearth Tax (1662–1672) across the Salford, Blackburn, and West Derby Hundreds.
  • Regional History: Published the comprehensive Rochdale History regional study, tracing the family's transition from 14th-century manorial tenants to key figures in the town's growth and industrial evolution.
  • Industrial Infrastructure: Added the Holt Town Mills study, charting urban migration to Manchester and Ancoats. This includes a complete, chronological transcript of historical Trade Directories (1780s–1800s) at the bottom of the page.
  • Estate Records: Expanded the Historic Houses & Estates section, mapping out architectural details, land boundaries, and ownership transitions of ancestral Holt seats.

March 2026

  • Legacies & Wealth: Restructured the probate section into a broader Legacies framework to encompass complex estate distributions:
    • Launched RSLC Wills & Administrations, featuring abstracted testaments from the Record Society of Lancashire and Cheshire.
    • Published Legacy Inquisitions (Inquisitiones Post Mortem), mapping out land-holding proofs, death dates, and direct legal heirs under the Crown.
  • Parish Registers: Transcribed and indexed the Manchester Cathedral Burials, unlocking vital 17th-, 18th-, and 19th-century urban burial records for the family.

February 2026

  • The Epoch Pages: Commenced development and rolled out the complete suite of historical **Epoch Pages**. This structural update allows researchers to navigate the Holt family timeline sequentially through major socio-economic eras—from the medieval period through the industrial rise.
  • Manorial Administration: Published the Manorial Records & Court Rolls portal, tracking tenancy disputes, fines, and land transfers from the Halmote and Manor courts.

January 2026

  • Etymology & Foundation: Published the foundational Surname Origins page, analyzing the earliest topographic, linguistic, and geographic markers of the Holt name in Lancashire.

December 2025

  • Website: Migration and modernisation of exisiting Holt Ancestry website to Github. The start of updating the current pages and adding new content.

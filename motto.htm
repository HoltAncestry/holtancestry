Mottoes used by the Holt Family

It is not known the true origin of mottoes. The words of the motto are usually emblazoned on an open scroll at the base of the heraldric shield. The words may be in latin, french, german or english and often refer to a dramatic life event of the family and of scriptural or proverbial nature.

DUM SPIRO SPERO
While I breath I hope

QUOD VULT, VALDE VULT
What he wishes, he wishes fervently

VINCERE EST VIVERE
To conquer is to live
Holt of Cheetham, Bts, Smyth 143 Sq. RAF

CERTUM PETE FINEM
Aim at a sure end Holt of Liverpool

EXALTAVIT HUMILES
He hath exalted the humble
Holte of Erdington

UT SANEM VULNERO
I wound to heal Crest: A spearhead. This motto probably alludes to the spear of Achilles, which had the power of curing as well as of inflicting wounds.

CHE SARA SARA
What will be will be Sir John Holt of Gristlehurst

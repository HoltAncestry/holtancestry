Francis HOLT (1380)
Leith the body of.. of LOWERPLACE.. 1731 and in the 70..
Also Elizabeth.. in the 64th year of ..

Also here.. the body of Francis HOLT the son of
Francis HOLT of LOWERPLACE the 2nd September 1771
in the 21st year of his age.

Also Francis HOLT of ROCHDALE plumber who
departed this life the 3rd of May 1795 aged
42 years.

Also Alice HOLT his sister who departed this
life the 16th day of April 1805 in the 59th
year of her age.

Also in memory of John HOLT who died October
11th 1821 aged 67 years.

Also Sam the son of Saml. STANDRING tailor of
YORKSHIRE STREET who died July 1st 1810 aged
1 year and 6 months.

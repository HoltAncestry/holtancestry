Rochdale
William Holt (1572)
Here lies the body of William HOLT of BLACKWATER
cardmaker who departed this life April the 26th
1759 in the 24 year of his age.

Also John Chadwick of PACKER STREET who died
September the 30th 1837 in the 81st year of his age.

Also of Betty the wife of John CHADWICK HOLT of
PACKER STREET who was buried 13th august 1785 in
the 27th year of her age.

Also of Ann the wife of the above John Chadwick
who departed this life the 1st day of June 1798
aet 37.

Also of Maley the wife of the above John CHADWICK
who departed this life the 18th day of January 1825
aged 76 yrs.

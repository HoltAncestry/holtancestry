Rochdale
Ann Holt (1210)
Here resteth the body of Ann the wife of
Joseph HOLT of MOORSIDE who departed this
life the 10th day of June 1799 in the 22nd
year of her age.

Also Mary his second wife died June 28th
1817 aged 50 years.

Also the said Joseph HOLT late of WELLFIELD
HALL died March 25th 1823 aged 58 years.

Also of Mary, their daughter and wife of John
MILLS of LOWERPLACE who departed this life on
the 28th of September 1845 aged 46 years.

Also of Alice the daughter of John and Mary
MILLS who died November 4th 1852 aged 24 years.

Also James their son who died 26th of April
1854 aged 54 years.

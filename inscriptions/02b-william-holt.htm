Harpurhey Cemetery,
Manchester Monumental Inscriptions
No 4301 NC
IN AFFECTIONATE
REMEMBRANCE OF
Henry Holt who died
September 10th 1861 Age 51 years
Thy voice is now silent thy heart is now cold
Where thy smile and thy welcome oft met us of old
We miss thee and mourn thee in silence unseen
We dwell on the memory of joys that have been

Also Emma Holt Granddaughter
of above who died October 19th 1867
Aged 2 years 3 months

Also James Holt Son of above
who died September 12th 1869 Aged 32 Years

Also William Holt Son of above
who died September 3rd 1924
Aged 85 years

AT REST

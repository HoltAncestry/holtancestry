Rochdale
Monumental Inscriptions
Jane Holt (1495)
Here resteth the body of Jane the wife
of John HOLT who died March 11th 1750
in the ye 32yr of her age.

Also 2 of her children.

Also the above named John Holt who
departed this Life October 31st 1783
aged 67 years.

Also Jane the Daughter of Major SCHOLFIELD
who departed this life May 26th 1799 in the
20th Year of her age.

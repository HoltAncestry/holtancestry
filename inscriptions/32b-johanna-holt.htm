Rochdale Monumental Inscriptions
Johanna HOLT (1447)
Here resteth the body of Joanna the daughter
of John HOLT of CASTLETON Mercht who died
30th of August 1782 aged seven months.

Also John his son who died 22nd of February
1787 aged seven months.

Also Sarah his daughter who died 10th of March
1791 in the eleventh year of her age.

Also in memory of Johanna HOLT the daughter of
John HOLT of CROSS FILED who died at BATH the
16th June 1807 in her 23 year.

Also Lies here interred John Holt (father of
the above children ) who departed this life
the 4th day of May 1808 aged 60.

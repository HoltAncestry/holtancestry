Rochdale Monumental Inscriptions
Mally Holt (129)
Here resteth the body of Mally daughter of
Eli HOLT of Bridge-hall Mill Fuller, who
departed this life the 27th of April 1788
in the 4th year of her age

The lord took her from the evil to come because
her soul was precious in his sight.

Also Sarah his daughter who departed this life
the 30th March 1800 in the 19th year of her age.

Also of the above named Eli HOLT who departed
this Life the 2nd of April 1828 aged 77 years.

Afflictions I have long time bore, physicians
were in vain, till god was pleased to take my
soul and cased me of my pain.

Rochdale Monumental Inscriptions
Richard HOLT (1516)
Here Resteth the body of Richard HOLT
who died 19th of February, 1771 aged
82 years.

Also Betty the wife of Richard HOLT who
died June 7th 1763 aged 69 years.

Also Betty HOLT died January 24th 1754
aged 20 yrs.

Also Ellen Daughter of Robert HOLT who
died Aug 29th 1805 aged 20 yrs.

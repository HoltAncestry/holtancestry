Rochdale
Robert HOLT (1012)
Here resteth the body of Mr. Robert HOLT
late Steward to Lady BLAND who departed
this life February 28th 1765 in the
67th year of his age.

Also the body of James Holt of YORKSHIRE
STREET who departed this life June 2nd
1784 aged 44 years.
He liv’d respected and dy,d lamented.
Also seven of his children.

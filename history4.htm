1500–1549

This section summarises the documentary references to the Holt family found in the Victoria History of the County of Lancaster (1906). These entries reflect landholding, tenancies, and legal transactions recorded in early Tudor Lancashire.

1500–1549

1500 James Holt (husband of Isabel) and others claimed a right of toll from all who crossed the Mersey between Runcorn and Thelwall (p. 112).

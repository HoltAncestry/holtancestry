Holt Family Biographical Index

This index provides an overview of notable individuals bearing the Holt surname, grouped by occupation and sphere of influence. It serves as a navigational gateway to extended biographical accounts, complementing the Houses & Halls section and the wider historical framework of HoltAncestry. Each entry links to a full profile where available.

Judges, Lawyers & Public Office

Holts who served in the legal profession or held positions of public authority.

  • Sir John Holt, Chief Justice — One of the most influential jurists in English legal history.
  • James Maden Holt — Barrister and Conservative MP for North-East Lancashire.
  • Sir Richard Durning Holt — Businessman and Liberal MP; prominent Liverpool figure.
  • Parlimentarian Holts — Many Holts served in parliament.

Merchants, Industrialists & Business Figures

Holts involved in trade, manufacturing, commerce, and the development of regional industries.

  • George Holt — Liverpool merchant, philanthropist, and founder of Sudley House.
  • John Holt of Liverpool — Merchant and founder of the John Holt & Co. trading enterprise.
  • John Holt of Portsmouth — Merchant and shipowner associated with Portsmouth’s maritime trade.
  • Sir Edward & Joseph Holt — Brewer and founder of the Joseph Holt Brewery, Manchester.
  • David Holt — Manchester mill owner and civic reformer shaping industry, education, and community welfare.
  • Joseph Holt — Established the Dock ship building company in Whitby.

Aristocracy & Hereditary Titles

Holt individuals who held hereditary honours such as baronetcies, knighthoods with hereditary succession, or similar titled status.

  • Sir Thomas Holt of Aston — the 1st Baronet of Aston, created in 1619 during the reign of James I. Lead the Holt family through early Stuart turmoil.

Landowners, Gentry & Estate Holders

Holts associated with estates, halls, and landed interests across Lancashire, Yorkshire, and other counties.

  • Robert Holt of Stubley — Landowner of Stubley Hall; central figure in the Rochdale Holt lineage.
  • Sir Thomas Holt of Gristlehurst — Landowner of Gristlehurst Hall; knighthood in 1544.

Military Service & Defence

Holts who served in the armed forces or held military commissions.

Clergy, Scholars & Learned Professions

Holts connected with the Church, universities, or scholarly pursuits.

  • William Holt, the Jesuit — Priest, diplomat, and influential figure in late Tudor religious politics.

Social Reform, Co‑operation & Community Leadership

Holts associated with social movements, co‑operative societies, or community development.

  • Rochdale Pioneers — Includes Holt family involvement in the early co‑operative movement.

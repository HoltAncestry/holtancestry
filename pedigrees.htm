The Heralds Visitations - Holt Pedigrees

The Heralds' visitations are a collection of pedigrees of families that have the right to bear arms. These were mainly recorded between the 16th and the 17th century to regulate the use of arms. The families had their arms recorded which detailed the quartering to which they were entitled. The pedigrees included the main line of descent and some family offshoots so that the route by which the quartered arms had been acquired was documented.

Stirnet
Stirnet (stirnet.com) is a long-established, independent online genealogy resource specializing in detailed pedigrees (family trees/ancestral lines) for British and Irish families. It covers thousands of families, with extensive coverage of gentry, noble, landed, and armigerous (coat-of-arms bearing) families, as well as many lesser-known ones. The pedigrees are compiled by pulling together and cross-referencing data from a wide range of published secondary sources (e.g., Visitations, Burke's Peerage, county histories, older genealogical works, and similar compilations).

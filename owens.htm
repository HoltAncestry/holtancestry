The Owen Manuscript

The Owen MSS consist of eighty volumes, all except one being either in folio or quarto, most of them being ordinary commercial account books and ledgers. In these volumes Mr Owen has drawn or written, in a particularly neat hand, material for local history which may be roughly grouped under five headings:

  1. Monumental inscriptions
  2. Parish registers
  3. Genealogical memoranda
  4. Architecture and archaeology
  5. Miscellaneous historical notes

The bulk of the Owen MSS refers to South‑East Lancashire and East Cheshire. Some of the Monumental Inscriptions recorded by John Owen circa 1880 for the name of HOLT are detailed below.

St Mary's Church, The Parsonage, Manchester

St Mary's Church was built in 1756 and enjoyed the patronage of Manchester's more affluent society — merchants and businessmen, including people from other cities such as London, Bath, Bristol, Liverpool, and Brussels. Extracted from Volume 29, pages 173–336.
